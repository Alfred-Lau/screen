# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, generous about people.
- Assume good faith. Ask what someone meant before assuming the worst reading.
- Give and accept technical criticism on its merits.
- Respect that maintainers have limited time and may decline a change.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile linked
in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), or use GitHub's private reporting form. Do not
open a public issue. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that
violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent ban.
